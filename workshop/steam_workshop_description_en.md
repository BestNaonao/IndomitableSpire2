# AZUR LANE: TASHKENT MOD

The Northern Parliament destroyer Tashkent from *Azur Lane* joins the Spire. Load ammunition, control your Distance, mark priority targets, and launch torpedoes that strike at exactly the right moment.

This mod requires 【BaseLib 3.4.5 or later】.

![Mod Content](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BestNaonao/IndomitableSpire2/branch_tashkent/workshop/images/tashkent_workshop_content.png)

## CORE MECHANICS

### AMMO & LOAD

Ammo cards display their current Ammo and maximum Ammo. 【Load】 adds Ammo to a card. Ammo is retained between combats, making long-term resource planning an important part of a run.

### FIRE & FOCUS FIRE

During combat, each Ammo spent triggers the effect written after 【Fire】 once.

When a card with 【Focus Fire X】 is played, choosing to spend at least X Ammo activates its additional effect.

### TORPEDO

Torpedoes automatically strike the enemy with the most 【Mark】 at the end of a future turn. Their damage is increased by the target's Mark, while their arrival time is determined by your Distance when they are launched:

- Distance 【4–5】: strikes at the end of the current turn
- Distance 【2–3】: strikes at the end of the next turn
- Any other Distance: strikes at the end of the turn after next

### DISTANCE

Distance represents your position relative to the enemy and ranges from 【-5 to 5】. Each point of positive Distance increases damage dealt by 【20%】 and damage taken by 【10%】. Negative Distance reduces both your damage dealt and damage taken.

Distance also determines how long torpedoes take to reach their targets. Charge forward for greater damage and faster impacts, or retreat to fight more safely.

### MARK

Mark is a debuff triggered by Attack damage. Whenever the target takes Attack damage, it takes additional damage equal to its current Mark, then loses 1 Mark. Torpedo damage also gains a bonus from the target's Mark.

## EXTRA FEATURES

- Switch between 【4 Tashkent outfits】 from the character selection screen.
- Multiplayer pointing and rock-paper-scissors models can use either the standard hand models or alternate leg models.
- Distance interacts with the Act 2 【Sandworm】 boss. Charging reduces its sandpit counter, while attempts to pull Tashkent into the sandpit can be converted into a damage advantage; retreating reverses the interaction.
- Distance also interacts with the Act 2 【Emperor Crab】 boss. Charging toward the left is equivalent to retreating from enemies on the right, and vice versa.
- The original colorless card 【The Bomb】 is converted into a torpedo and benefits from Tashkent's torpedo-related bonuses.
- Torpedoes, Poseidon Form, Warp Drive, and other abilities include dedicated combat effects.
- Supports Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean.
